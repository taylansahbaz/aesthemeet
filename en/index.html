Frequently Asked Questions

Is hair transplantation permanent?

+

Yes, hair transplantation is permanent. Hair taken from the donor area is genetically permanent and lasts a lifetime.

How long until hair starts growing?

+

Transplanted hair sheds after 3-4 weeks and starts growing again after 3 months. Growth continues up to 6 months. Full results are visible at 12 months.

Is there any pain?

+

Since local anesthesia is applied, no pain is felt during the procedure. Mild discomfort may be experienced but there is no pain.

How many days until I can return to work?

+

You can return to light work after 3-4 days. You can start heavy physical activities after 2-3 weeks.

Are there any contraindications?

+

The patient must have a sufficient donor area and be in good general health. A detailed evaluation is made during the consultation.
